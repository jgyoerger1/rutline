Support

Rutline is built and supported by one hunter in Northeast Ohio.

Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at github.com/jgyoerger1/rutline/issues. Say what phone you use, what you tapped and what you expected. Issues are answered within a few days, faster in season.

Common questions

  • The map doesn't show where I am. Allow location for Rutline in your phone's Settings. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Rutline → While Using.
  • The blood light shows a black screen. Allow the camera the same way. The light works best at night with a flashlight held low and to the side.
  • Property lines look off. Lines come from county records and are approximate, not a survey. Confirm a boundary with the county or the landowner before you hunt it.
  • A county shows no owner names. Some counties don't publish them. The parcel sheet links to that county's records where it can.
  • How do I move my pins to a new phone? Sign in on both phones and they sync, or use Settings → Your data → Backup and Restore.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Danger zone. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
